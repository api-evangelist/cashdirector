# CashDirector

> AI-powered financial management for small businesses, delivered white-label to banks (B2B2C). Embeds banking + accounting automation — invoicing, expense tracking, cash flow forecasting, receivables, OCR document processing — inside partner banks' online platforms as a "beyond banking" daily digital business assistant. Reports 250,000+ users across six major European bank partnerships. A Techstars portfolio company. No public developer portal or open API surface — integration is delivered privately to bank partners.
